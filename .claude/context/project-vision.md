# Project Vision

## Vision Statement
To democratize content creation by making high-quality blogging accessible to everyone, regardless of their writing abilities, language proficiency, or technical expertise, through intelligent AI assistance that enhances rather than replaces human creativity.

## Mission
Prompted Blog empowers individuals to share their ideas with the world by providing an intuitive, AI-assisted writing platform that removes traditional barriers to content creation while maintaining authenticity and personal voice.
